Privacy Policy For Call Spam Blocker

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

1. On-Device Processing (No Cloud Storage)

Our core philosophy is privacy first. Call Spam Blocker processes all incoming phone numbers locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your call logs, incoming phone numbers, or blocklists on any external servers or cloud databases.

2. Permissions We Request and Why

To block spam effectively, our app requires specific system permissions. All data accessed by these permissions stays strictly on your phone:

  • Call Screening / Phone State: Required to detect incoming caller IDs in real-time and automatically silence or reject numbers on your blocklist.
  • Contacts (Optional): If granted, this allows the app to check incoming numbers against your address book locally so we never accidentally block your friends or family. Your contact list is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Call Logs: Used solely to display your blocked history within the app interface.

3. Third-Party Analytics

We may use privacy-compliant, anonymized analytics tools (such as Google Firebase Analytics) to track app crashes and basic usage statistics (e.g., app launches). This data contains zero personal information, phone numbers, or call data.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not target and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13 (or 18 depending on your region). We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@sojiha.com
